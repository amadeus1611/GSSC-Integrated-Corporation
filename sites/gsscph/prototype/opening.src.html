Programmes rarely fail for lack of suppliers. They fail in the gaps between them, where each party answers for a slice of the outcome and no one answers for the whole. GSSC is structured to occupy that gap: one programme lead, one scoped quotation, and one controlled path from approval to turnover.

II—Engagement

From requirement to turnover, one controlled path.

Five stages, one programme lead and one acceptance gate. At each gate you receive one document, and together they make one file: what was asked, what was quoted, what was accepted, what was done and what was handed over.

  1. i

    Requirement

    A short scope conversation: category, site, timeline and the constraint that shapes the work.

  2. ii

    Scoped quotation

    Bundled work packages, stated assumptions and approved Non-VAT pricing, reconciled to the request.

  3. iii

    Acceptance

    One acceptance gate. Mobilisation is scheduled only against the accepted scope.

  4. iv

    Delivery

    One programme lead coordinates every discipline on site and reports against the quotation.

  5. v

    Turnover

    Documented handover and close-out, filed to the same standard as the quotation.

III—Economics

What coordinating it yourself costs.

A client who buys each discipline separately also buys the work of holding them together: the purchase orders, the invoices, the hours on the phone and the rework that falls between trades. Move the figures to your programme.
