Volume I · Company Profile · Institutional Introduction

A Single Point of Accountability for Integrated Programs.

GSSC Integrated Corporation is a super-sourcing, management consultancy, and business support platform. It coordinates sourcing, site execution, supply programs and bespoke requirements under one governed programme, so a client carries one relationship and one line of accountability from the first scope conversation to turnover.

Prepared for · Institutional Review Issued · Iloilo City, Philippines

Programs rarely fail for lack of suppliers. They fail in the gaps between them, where each party answers for a slice of the outcome and no one answers for the whole. GSSC is structured to occupy that gap.

Every engagement runs under GSSC governance: one programme lead, one scoped quotation, and one controlled path from approval to turnover. The pages that follow set out who GSSC is, what it coordinates, who holds authority for the corporation, and how an engagement begins.

Coordination, integration, sourcing, and accountable delivery across varied requirements.
— GSSC Integrated Corporation · Positioning

GSSC is registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission and the Bureau of Internal Revenue as a Non-VAT domestic corporation, with its principal office at Iloilo Business Park, Iloilo City.

Across equipment, water, diesel, waste, civil, signage, furniture, landscaping, mechanical, electrical, food service and consultancy requirements, the arrangement does not change: GSSC answers for the programme as a whole.

Who We Are

Positioning, structure and the three service pillars

GSSC Integrated Corporation is a super-sourcing, management consultancy, and business support platform delivering single-point accountability across programmes. It does not position as a single-trade supplier: it coordinates, integrates and sources across varied requirements, and remains the one accountable counterparty to the client throughout.

01 Super-Sourcing
Strategic sourcing and procurement across categories. GSSC identifies, qualifies and coordinates the right supply for each requirement, so the client deals with one counterparty instead of a shifting list of vendors.
Strategic sourcing · Procurement · Programme integration
Coverage · All programme families 14 categories
02 Management Consultancy
Advisory and coordination support for programmes that span disciplines: scoping, sequencing, documentation and controlled hand-offs between the parties a programme depends on.
Scoping · Sequencing · Programme governance
Registered activity · PSIC 70200
03 Business Support
Operational support that keeps a programme moving between milestones: submittals, delivery records, turnover documentation and a defined continuity window after acceptance.
Documentation · Turnover records · Continuity support
Registered activity · PSIC 82990

Whatever the category of work, the client-facing structure is the same: one scoped quotation, one programme lead, and one acceptance gate before anything proceeds.
