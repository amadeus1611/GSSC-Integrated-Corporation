Volume I · Institutional Introduction · GSSC Integrated Corporation

A single point of accountability for integrated programs.

Super-sourcing, management consultancy, and business support — coordinated under one accountable roof, from Iloilo City, Philippines.

Prepared for · Institutional Review Registration · SEC · Non-VAT

GSSC Integrated Corporation exists to remove a specific kind of friction: the one a client absorbs when a single program depends on several separately managed suppliers, each accountable for a slice of the outcome and none for the whole of it. GSSC sits in that gap as one accountable partner.

Rather than positioning as a single-trade supplier, GSSC coordinates, integrates, and sources across varied requirements — spanning strategic sourcing, site execution, specialty supply programs, and open-category bespoke work — so a client manages one relationship instead of a shifting list of vendors.

Coordination, integration, sourcing, and accountable delivery across varied requirements.— GSSC positioning · differentiator

The pages that follow set out how that accountability is structured in practice: the categories GSSC coordinates, the officers who hold recorded authority for the corporation, and the single point of contact through which every engagement runs.
