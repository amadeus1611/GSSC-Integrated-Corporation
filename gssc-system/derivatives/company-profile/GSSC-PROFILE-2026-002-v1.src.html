Overview

Who we are, how the profile is organized, and what each section covers.

GSSC Integrated Corporation is a super-sourcing, management consultancy, and business support platform delivering single-point accountability across programs. Rather than positioning as a single-trade supplier, GSSC coordinates, integrates, and sources across varied requirements — so a client manages one accountable partner instead of a shifting list of vendors.
