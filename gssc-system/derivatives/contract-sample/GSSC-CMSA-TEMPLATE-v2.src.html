IArticle

Scope of Services

Services are defined per engagement, never in this Agreement

1.1Clause

Services under issued instruments

GSSC shall provide the services described in each Statement of Work (“SOW”), Purchase Order (“PO”) or Notice to Proceed (“NTP”) issued under and referencing this Agreement (the “Services”). Each accepted instrument forms part of, and is governed by, this Agreement.

1.2Clause

Acceptance before performance

No Services are authorised, and no obligation to perform arises, until the applicable instrument has been issued and accepted in writing by [acceptance mechanism — countersigned quotation, PO number or NTP date].

1.3Clause

Order of precedence

Engagement-specific scope, deliverables, schedule and specifications are stated in the governing SOW, PO or NTP. Where an instrument conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement prevails unless the instrument expressly names the clause it varies.

Term and renewal

This Agreement commences on the date first written above and continues for [initial term], renewing automatically for successive [renewal period] periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least [notice period] days before the current term ends.

2.2Clause

Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement for a material breach not cured within [cure period] days of written notice, or for convenience on [notice period] days’ written notice.

2.3Clause

Engagements in progress

Fees

Fees for each engagement are as stated in the applicable SOW, PO or NTP, in Philippine Pesos on a Non-VAT basis. GSSC is a Non-VAT registered taxpayer and invoices in its current BIR-authorised invoice format.

3.2Clause

Payment tiers

Unless the signed instrument states otherwise, payment follows GSSC’s approved payment tiers as agreed for the Client: [downpayment, milestone and balance terms approved at signing].

3.3Clause

Late payment

Amounts unpaid when due accrue interest at [rate] per [period], or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

3.4Clause

Taxes and withholding

Each Party bears its own taxes. Withholding tax, where legally required on a payment, is applied under current BIR regulations and supported by the corresponding certificate of tax withheld.
